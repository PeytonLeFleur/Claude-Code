**Company:** NioCorp Developments Ltd. (NASDAQ: NB; TSX: NB)

**Research date:** 2026-06-21
**Prepared from:** Public primary sources — NioCorp SEC filings (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, DEF 14A, Form 4), the June 2022 NI 43-101 / S-K 1300 Feasibility Study and its press releases, NioCorp press releases and the May 2026 investor presentation, U.S. EXIM Bank disclosures, and supporting secondary coverage where flagged.

> **How to read this document**
> Each answer states whether the figure is **DISCLOSED** (found in a primary company source), **PARTIALLY DISCLOSED** (some elements public, others not), or **NOT DISCLOSED** (the company has not publicly answered). Confidence notes and source URLs are included. No figures were fabricated; where a number lives only inside the full technical report or requires EDGAR XBRL extraction, that is stated.

> 3. **The entire capital stack hinges on the U.S. EXIM Bank debt package (up to ~$800M requested).** Detailed loan terms and a firm board-decision date are not public.

## SECTION 3 — Resource, Mine Plan & Technical Risk (Q21–Q30)

### Q21. When will the updated feasibility study be released?
**NOT PRECISELY DISCLOSED — repeatedly soft-guided, no firm date, and effectively slipped.** Engaged engineering firms in April 2025; the 2025 Year-in-Review lists "publishing an updated Feasibility Study" as a generic **2026 priority** with no deadline; analyst coverage framed a Q1 2026 inflection. As of 2026-06-21 the updated FS does not appear in NioCorp's news-release list. *Confidence: High that no firm public date exists.* Source: [Drilling to support updated FS](https://www.niocorp.com/niocorp-to-initiate-drilling-program-at-elk-creek-project-in-order-to-support-updated-feasibility-study/).

### Q22. Specific assumptions being changed in the updated mine plan vs. 2022
**PARTIALLY DISCLOSED (qualitative).** Key changes: (a) **add magnetic rare-earth oxides** (Nd, Pr, Dy, Tb) as products; (b) titanium product shifting to **titanium tetrachloride (TiCl₄)**; (c) both **ferroniobium and niobium oxide** as products; (d) **twin mine ramps (declines) + Railveyor** electric haulage replacing the twin vertical shafts; (e) a "new, more efficient production process." Changes to ramp-up schedule/throughput (prior 2,762 t/d) are **not disclosed.**

### Q23. Drilling results still pending and how they could change reserves/grades/mine life/economics
**PARTIALLY DISCLOSED — program scope yes, quantitative outcomes no.** The **Department of War / Pentagon-funded in-fill program** (completed Sept 8, 2025: 17 HQ holes, ~11,521 m) and assays of 7,000+ core samples are being incorporated into an updated resource/reserve estimate and mine plan. Designed to improve model confidence, convert resource/reserve categories, and generate **rare-earth assays**. **Actual updated grades, tonnages, and REE assay values are NOT disclosed** (were pending at last release). This directly affects EXIM's reserve requirement. *Confidence: High on scope; outcomes genuinely undisclosed.* Source: [Pentagon-funded drilling complete](https://www.niocorp.com/niocorp-completes-pentagon-funded-drilling-campaign-at-elk-creek-critical-minerals-project/).

### Q24. Expected resource conversion (indicated→measured, probable→proven)
**DIRECTION DISCLOSED, MAGNITUDE NOT.** NioCorp says drilling is designed to convert "a portion of" Indicated → Measured and Probable Reserves → Proven, but **no tonnage or percentage is published.** Baseline (2022 FS): Probable Reserves **31.7 Mt @ 0.79% Nb₂O₅, 71.6 g/t Sc, 2.81% TiO₂**; Indicated 90.9 Mt; Inferred 133.6 Mt — with **zero Proven/Measured** as of the FS, so any conversion would be the first. *Confidence: High that magnitude is undisclosed.*

### Q25. Biggest unresolved underground geotechnical risk
**NOT EXPLICITLY DISCLOSED as a named risk.** Mining method is **long-hole stoping with paste backfill**, relying on rock-mass competency and backfill performance; the 2025 program included ~1,950 m of geomechanical drilling specifically for this. The **shaft→twin-ramp/Railveyor switch** introduces new decline-stability and ground-support considerations. No ranked geotechnical risk register is published. *Confidence: High that no formal register is public.*

### Q26. Groundwater control / dewatering plan and downside risk
**PARTIALLY DISCLOSED.** The 2022 FS included a hydrogeology program and allocated **~$100M to "Mine Water Management"** — a material line item signaling significant dewatering needs (orebody sits beneath water-bearing strata). Piezometric monitoring devices were installed after the 2023 geotech campaign. **Downside/sensitivity if dewatering is harder than expected is NOT quantified.** *Confidence: Medium.*

### Q27. Technical risk of switching to twin ramps + Railveyor
**DISCLOSED as a benefit, not framed as risk.** In 2024 NioCorp named **Railveyor** (electric light-rail bulk haulage) the preferred solution, replacing the twin-shaft design, citing lower initial capital (~$188M), simpler infrastructure, shorter schedule, all-electric operation. The Dec 2025 portal approval (~$44.6M) physically enables the declines. **Risk (analyst-level):** Railveyor's throughput at Elk Creek's ~2,762 t/d, decline haulage over depth, ventilation, and ramp-up reliability must be re-validated in the updated FS. Only a **2024 scoping study** is referenced — **no feasibility-grade trade-off study is yet public.** *Confidence: High that the company frames it positively; the independent validation is pending.*

### Q28. Metallurgical test work proving commercial-scale recovery
**DISCLOSED — but only at demonstration / pilot scale (not commercial).** Work by **L3 Process Development** (Trois-Rivières, Quebec) on ~3,000 kg of Elk Creek core:
- **Niobium & titanium:** "processing breakthrough" (2023); titanium recovery shown capable of roughly **doubling** vs. FS.
- **Rare earths:** HCl leaching 86–95% dissolution; solvent-extraction loading up to 99%; projected **>92%** overall recovery for the four magnetic REEs at commercial purity.
- **Scandium:** ~92% high-purity Sc₂O₃ at demo scale; **scandium metal produced at pilot scale** (Aug 2023) for the Al-Sc master-alloy initiative.
*Confidence: High on the demonstration results.* Source: [Niobium/titanium breakthrough](https://www.niocorp.com/niocorp-achieves-processing-breakthrough-in-demonstration-plant-testing-of-niobium-and-titanium-production/).

### Q29. Assumed recovery rates per product and downside cases
**BASE RATES DISCLOSED; DOWNSIDE CASES NOT.**
| Product | 2022 FS recovery | Demo-plant update |
|---|---|---|
| Niobium (Nb₂O₅) | **82.4%** | higher recoveries demonstrated (not yet a published FS number) |
| Scandium (Sc) | **93.1%** | ~92% high-purity Sc₂O₃ at demo |
| Titanium (TiO₂) | **40.3%** | demonstrated potential to ~double |
| Magnetic REEs | N/A (not in FS) | projected **>92%** at demo |

**No recovery-rate sensitivity/downside table is published.** Titanium's 40.3% is the most conservative assumption with the most upside and uncertainty until the updated FS locks a number. *Confidence: High.*

### Q30. Which part of the flowsheet is NOT yet proven at commercial scale?
**Effectively the entire integrated hydrometallurgical flowsheet remains pre-commercial.** All recovery work to date is **demonstration/pilot scale** (~3,000 kg batches), not continuous commercial operation. The clearest unproven elements: (a) **rare-earth separation** into individual high-purity magnetic oxides via solvent extraction (newest, added after the 2022 FS); (b) **titanium-to-TiCl₄** (new product form); (c) **scandium metal / Al-Sc master alloy** (pilot scale only); (d) **integration of all unit operations in continuous steady-state at ~2,762 t/d** (never run). No formal technology-readiness statement is published. *Confidence: High that nothing has run at commercial scale; "weakest link" framing is inference.*

### Q31. Which product generates the most project value?
**DISCLOSED: scandium oxide, not niobium.** Despite being marketed as a niobium/"superalloy" project, the economics are scandium-weighted: Sc₂O₃ ~66% of FS annual product revenue vs. FeNb ~32% and TiO₂ ~2%. *Confidence: High on revenue split; Medium on per-product NPV (NioCorp does not publish a clean per-product NPV breakdown).*

### Q32. % of revenue dependent on scandium demand developing
**~66% of FS base-case product revenue ($378.3M of ~$572M annual)** — derived from disclosed per-product revenue; **not stated by the company as a "scandium-dependence" headline.** *Confidence: High (arithmetic from primary figures).*

### Q33. Real end buyers for scandium and committed volumes
**PARTIALLY DISCLOSED — and this is a central risk.** Only **up to 12 t/yr (~12% of output)** is under a **binding** sales agreement with **Traxys** — a trading/marketing intermediary, **not an end-user**. A non-binding term sheet (Apr 9, 2026) would have Traxys take the remainder on a **best-efforts (not take-or-pay)** basis. **True end-buyers are not firmly committed.** NioCorp is instead building its own downstream demand (acquired **FEA Materials LLC**, Dec 2025; partners with Nanoscale/Creative Engineers under a ~$10M DoD award; Lockheed Martin Skunk Works collaboration) to *create* Al-Sc master-alloy demand. *Confidence: High that only ~12% is contracted, on soft terms.*

> **Market-size context (demand-creation risk):** Global scandium oxide output is tiny — roughly **30–40 t/yr** (USGS-based). NioCorp alone plans **~104 t/yr**, i.e., **~2.5–3× current world output**. There is no existing market large enough to absorb Elk Creek scandium at FS volumes/prices; this is the project's central demand-creation risk and the reason for vertical integration.

### Q34. Minimum ferroniobium price needed
**NOT DISCLOSED.** FS uses $39.60/kg with ±30% sensitivity, but no breakeven/minimum threshold is published.

### Q35. Minimum scandium price needed
**NOT DISCLOSED.** FS uses $3,675/kg with ±30% sensitivity; no breakeven figure. *Context: market scandia has historically traded volatile and often well below $3,675/kg — a key risk.*

### Q36. Minimum titanium product price needed
**NOT DISCLOSED.** FS uses $0.88/kg TiO₂; titanium is ~2% of revenue and economically immaterial.

### Q37. Are rare earths in the financed base case or upside?
**DISCLOSED: UPSIDE / OPTIONALITY — not in the financed 2022 FS base case.** NioCorp is still "conducting technical and economic analyses on the potential addition" of magnetic rare-earth oxides (NdPr, Dy, Tb). An updated NI 43-101 incorporating REEs is planned but not finalized. *Confidence: High.*

### Q38. Does the project justify its valuation without rare earths?
**YES per the 2022 FS** — the base-case NPV/IRR were generated with **zero REE revenue** (Nb+Sc+Ti only: pre-tax NPV ~$2.8B / after-tax ~$2.35B). **Caveat:** that base case is heavily scandium-dependent, and the *current investment thesis* increasingly leans on REE upside. *Interpretive on the current thesis; High on the FS fact.*

### Q39. Will NioCorp produce separated rare-earth oxides itself or via a third party?
**DISCLOSED (in-house intent):** NioCorp intends to **produce separated magnetic rare-earth oxides itself**, using the same Elk Creek hydrometallurgical flowsheet (demonstrated via its rare-earth-magnet recycling test work). No third-party separator/refiner is named. *Confidence: Medium-High (stated intent; commercial REE separation not yet built/proven at scale).*

### Q40. Which customers have completed technical qualification of Elk Creek material?
**NOT DISCLOSED that any customer has completed technical qualification.** Offtake counterparties are disclosed (Thyssen, Traxys, Stellantis term sheet) but qualification status is not — and the mine is not yet producing, so no actual Elk Creek product exists to qualify. *Confidence: High.*
